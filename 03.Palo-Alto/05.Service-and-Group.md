## Firewall Service and Service Groups:

In Palo Alto firewalls, **Service Objects** and **Service Groups** define the `TCP` or `UDP` ports used by applications and security policies. They help you control which network services are allowed through the firewall.


_Service Objects:_
- WEB-HTTP — TCP/80
- WEB-HTTPS — TCP/443
- APP-TCP-8080 — TCP/8080
- SSH-SERVICE — TCP/22



## Configure Service and Service Group on Firewall:


### Create Service:

1. Navigate to: `OBJECTS` - `Services` - click `+ Add`:
    - Name: `allow-http-https`
    - Description: 
    - Protocol: `TCP` | UDP
    - Destination Port: `80,443`
    - Source Port: N/A
    - Session Timeout: ✅ `Inherit from application` | Override
    - Tags: 
    - Click `OK`


2. Navigate to: `OBJECTS` - `Services` - click `+ Add`:
    - Name: `allow-ssh`
    - Description: 
    - Protocol: `TCP` | UDP
    - Destination Port: `22`
    - Source Port: N/A
    - Session Timeout: ✅ `Inherit from application` | Override
    - Tags: 
    - Click `OK`



### Create Service Groups:

1. Navigate to: `OBJECTS` - `Services Groups` - click `+ Add`:
    - Name: `custom-service-group`
    - SERVICE - click `+ Add`:
        - `allow-http-https`
        - `allow-ssh`
    - Click `OK`
