## Firewall Address and Address Groups:

In Palo Alto firewalls, `Address Objects` and `Address Groups` let you define IP addresses, subnets, ranges, and collections of hosts for reuse in security policies, NAT policies, and other supported configurations.



## Configure Address and Address Group on Firewall:


### Create Address:

1. Navigate to: `OBJECTS` - `Addresses` - click `+ Add`:
    - Name: host-13.4
    - Description: 
    - Type:` IP NetMask` : `192.168.13.4/32`
    - Click `OK`


2. Navigate to: `OBJECTS` - `Addresses` - click `+ Add`:
    - Name: host-13.5
    - Description: 
    - Type:` IP NetMask` : `192.168.13.5/32`
    - Click `OK`



### Create Static Address Group:

1. Navigate to: `OBJECTS` - `Address Groups` - click `+ Add`:
    - Name: `host-group-Lan-13`
    - Description: 
    - Type: `Static`
    - Addresses - click `+ Add`:
        - `host-13.4`
        - `host-13.5`
    - Click `OK`
